## Decision

AWO is preserved as a published methodology for reproducible AI–human research. It is not a current workflow engine or dependency of the Waveframe product runtime.

## Current boundary

AWO’s phases, artifacts, and contracts document the original research-method architecture. Current CRI-CORE evaluates execution-boundary admissibility and does not execute AWO workflows. Any future integration requires an explicit, versioned interface rather than relying on historical lineage.

This repository does not authorize, configure, or constrain the current Waveframe product merely through historical hierarchy claims. Current behavior is defined by the versioned contracts, source, tests, and releases of the active product repositories.

## Maintenance policy

Changes are limited to:

- correcting factual or citation errors;
- preserving published and DOI-backed artifacts;
- security, licensing, and repository-integrity maintenance;
- explicitly approved new research revisions; and
- recording a future versioned adoption decision, if one occurs.

Routine Waveframe product development does not require corresponding changes here.

## Historical language

Documents describing a direct ARI → AWO → Neurotransparency → CRI-CORE enforcement hierarchy capture the original Aurora architecture. They remain part of the research record, but this status document governs claims about the repositories’ present relationship to the Waveframe product.
